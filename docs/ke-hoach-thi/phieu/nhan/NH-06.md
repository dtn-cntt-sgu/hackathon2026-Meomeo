  - `prompts/questions.ts`: `questionsSchema(items: QuestionPlanItem[])`, `parseQuestionsRaw(json): { questions: GeneratedQuestionRaw[] } | null`, `buildNodeAnalysisText(node, analysis): string`, `buildQuestionsUserPrompt(graph, node, analysis, items, missed?): string`, `questionsRequest(session, graph, node, analysis, items, missed?): LlmRequest<{ questions: GeneratedQuestionRaw[] }>`.

  - `enrich.ts`: `findOpenRun(sessionId, nodeId): Promise<RunRecord | null>`, `toQuestionView(q): QuestionView` (không có đáp án khi câu chưa xong), `buildEnrichResponse(run, progress): EnrichNodeResponse`, `checkNodeAvailable(sessionId, userId, nodeId): Promise<NodeStateView>`, `loadNodeAnalysis(session, nodeId): Promise<NodeAnalysis>` (chưa có ⇒ `ERR_ANALYSIS_NOT_READY`), `loadUserSkills(userId)`, `createStepRefs(traceSteps): RunStepRef[]`, `createRun(sessionId, nodeId, mode, questions, steps, forbiddenExternalKeywords): Promise<RunRecord>`.

Step 4 `planNodeQuestions` ⇒ `generateQuestions`. Step 5 mọi câu `DROPPED` ⇒ `ERR_AI_REJECTED` (retryable). Step 6 `createRun`, cộng token. Step 7 trả `EnrichNodeResponse`.
